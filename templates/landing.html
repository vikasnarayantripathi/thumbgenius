What is ThumbGenius?

ThumbGenius is an AI-powered YouTube thumbnail intelligence platform. Generate viral thumbnails, predict CTR, analyze existing thumbnails, and get complete content packages with titles, hooks and SEO tags.

Is it free to try?

Yes! Free plan gives 3 title generations, 1 thumbnail analysis, and 1 viral blueprint per month — no credit card required.

Which languages are supported?

English, Hindi, Hinglish, Telugu, Tamil, Bengali, and Marathi — built specifically for Indian creators.

How does AI thumbnail generation work?

AI analyzes your topic, niche and style to generate a custom thumbnail using Google Imagen 4. Choose Clean, Text in Image, or Text Overlay modes.

India: UPI, Cards, NetBanking via Razorpay. Global: Stripe coming soon. All payments are secured.
